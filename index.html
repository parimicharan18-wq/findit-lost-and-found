QUICK START MANUAL

How to use FindIt.

Follow these steps to search, report, and share an item safely.

Back to top ↑
01

Look for a matching item

Use the search box to enter an item, category, or place. Filter by Found or Lost, then open a report to read its details. You can also use Scan QR to open a found-item listing.

02

Create a lost or found report

Choose I lost an item or I found an item. Add the item name, category, general location, date, and visible details. You can attach a photo; it will appear on your item card after saving.

03

Share details and arrange a return

Open your report and choose Copy share link or download its QR. Links include public listing details, but leave out photos and email. In this local demo, reports and contact information stay in this browser; a public service needs online hosting to coordinate contact across devices.

Safety tip: use a general location and keep one identifying detail private so the true owner can verify the item.
