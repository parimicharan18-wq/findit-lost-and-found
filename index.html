QUICK START MANUAL

How to use FindIt.

Follow these steps to search, report, and share an item safely.

Back to top ↑
01

Look for a matching item

Use the search box to enter an item, category, or place. Filter by Found or Lost, then open a report to read its details. You can also use Scan QR to open a found-item listing.

02

Create a lost or found report

Choose I lost an item or I found an item. Add the item name, category, general location, date, and visible details. Photos are optional; shared photos can be seen by anyone.

03

Share details and arrange a return

When the community board is configured, reports appear to visitors on other devices. Open a report to send a private message. The reporter reads it in My inbox in the same browser where they created the report; FindIt does not email notifications. Links leave out email and photos. Without a configured board, reports stay in this browser.

Safety tip: use a general location and keep one identifying detail private so the true owner can verify the item.
