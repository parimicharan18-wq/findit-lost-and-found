# FindIt launch kit

**Public link:** <https://parimicharan18-wq.github.io/findit-lost-and-found/>

## Who to reach first

Start with people who manage shared spaces and already handle misplaced belongings: campus and school offices, libraries, community centers, coworking spaces, and neighborhood groups. A small group that actually tries FindIt is more useful than posting the same link everywhere at once.

## Short description

FindIt gives communities one place to report lost and found items. Owners can register belongings and share QR tags; a finder scan opens a form to send the owner a report.

## Simple free launch sequence

1. Ask one campus office, library, or community group to try the app and give feedback.
2. Publish the LinkedIn post and share the Facebook copy only in groups where promotion is allowed.
3. Join relevant Product Hunt discussions, prepare screenshots, and submit the listing from the maker's personal account.
4. Use the Reddit draft only in a relevant community that allows creator posts; read its rules and ask moderators if unclear.
5. Reply to questions, collect feedback, and improve the app before repeating the announcement elsewhere.

## Claims and posting notes

- Describe the QR flow, finder report form, registered items, and owner dashboard.
- Do not advertise automatic email notifications or public phone/SMS sign-in until those external providers are configured.
- Be transparent about being the app creator. Do not buy votes or send mass direct messages.
- Tailor each post to the community instead of pasting identical promotions everywhere. Reddit explicitly warns against repetitive self-promotion and asks people to check community-specific rules: [Reddit spam guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam).
- Product Hunt allows makers to launch their own working product for free and recommends a clear product page, useful visuals, and genuine discussion: [Launch guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17350772-launch-guide), [How launches work](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734306-1-how-product-hunt-works).
