FindIt Privacy Notice

Updated November 6, 2026

FindIt helps people report lost or found items, register belongings, and return items through QR tags. This notice explains what information the service uses and how it is shared.

Information you provide

  • Owner accounts: your name, email address, mobile number, and authentication data. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides the basic account information needed to sign you in.
  • Registered items: item name, category, description, optional photo URL, status, and a QR tag. FindIt stores a hash of the private QR token; scanning a valid tag reveals the item details needed to help return it.
  • Finder reports: your name, email, mobile number, found location, and message. These details are stored with the item report and are visible to that item’s owner in the FindIt dashboard.
  • Community listings: details entered into a public lost or found listing, including any photo you attach, may be visible to visitors. Keep addresses, identity documents, and other sensitive details out of public listings.

How information is used

FindIt uses account and item information to provide registration, login, QR tags, dashboards, and finder reports. Finder contact information is shared with the owner of the matching registered item so they can arrange its return. If owner email notifications are configured, report details may also be sent to that owner by email. Phone sign-in and email delivery depend on external providers and may be unavailable until configured.

Service providers and storage

FindIt uses Supabase for authentication and the registered-item/report database, Google when you select Google sign-in, and GitHub Pages to host this website. Information sent through a provider is handled under that provider’s own privacy terms. FindIt does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Some preferences, sign-in session data, and reports created in local-only features may be stored in your browser. You can clear browser storage to remove that local data; doing so may sign you out or remove locally saved reports.

Retention and requests

Account and report information is kept while needed to operate FindIt. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account or associated information, contact FindIt support. We may need to verify that the request is yours.

Security

FindIt uses authenticated access controls so registered-item details and finder reports are available to their owner. A QR tag is a bearer link: anyone who scans or receives an active tag can view the item details encoded for return. Keep tags on the item and rotate a tag if it is lost or shared unintentionally.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as FindIt changes. The current version will be available on this page. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to FindIt support.
